## Remaining models

The other Nexus catalogue entries still need model-matched photographs. Do not substitute a visually similar appliance or a different model number.

## Clearance and implementation rule

Prefer a digital product-image pack supplied by Nexus Appliances or written permission from the image owner. Publicly accessible product photos are not automatically licensed for republication. Once cleared, copy approved originals into the Jaman Store product asset directory, optimize to WebP, update each product's local image path, and verify the product card and detail gallery on desktop and mobile.
