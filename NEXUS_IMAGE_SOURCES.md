## Publishing and implementation rule

Publicly accessible retailer photos are not automatically licensed for republication. Prefer an image pack supplied by Nexus or written permission from the image owner. Once cleared, copy approved originals into Jaman Store's own product asset directory, optimize to WebP, map each file to its exact model, and verify product cards and galleries on desktop and mobile. Do not hotlink retailer image URLs. Do not substitute a visually similar appliance or a different model number.
