Privacy Policy

Jaman Store, operated by Jaman Business Company, respects your privacy and handles customer information for the purposes of processing orders, payments, customer support and pickup coordination.

Information we collect

  • Full name, email address and phone number.
  • State and selected collection city.
  • Order, product, payment-reference and transaction information.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting us by email or WhatsApp.

We do not ask customers to enter a home delivery address because Jaman Store currently operates a pickup-only collection model.

Why we use your information

  • To process and confirm orders and payments.
  • To calculate the applicable collection and distribution charge.
  • To coordinate the customer's collection point and communicate order updates.
  • To provide customer support, returns and exchanges.
  • To maintain transaction records and meet applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

Service providers

Payment processing is provided through Paystack. Order and application data is stored and processed using our hosted database and application infrastructure. These providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services, subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

Security and retention

We use access controls, authenticated administration, server-side payment verification and other technical safeguards appropriate to the service. Customer information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for order administration, customer support, accounting, legal obligations and legitimate business records.

Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request information about personal data we hold about you and request correction, objection, restriction, portability or deletion where applicable. You may also lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

For privacy requests, contact Jamanbusinesscompany@gmail.com.

Cookies and local storage

The current Jaman Store storefront does not intentionally use advertising cookies. Your shopping cart is stored in your browser's local storage so that products remain in your cart while you browse. Third-party services such as payment providers may use their own technologies on their own domains.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, technology or legal obligations change. The current version will be published on this page.
